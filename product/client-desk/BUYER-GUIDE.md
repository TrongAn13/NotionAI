# Client Desk · Design / Web Studio

A project workspace for independent website, landing-page and brand designers.

## Your first project

1. Open **Client rooms** from Next opportunities. Create your client and enter the contact details you actually use.
2. In **Delivery studio → Project intake**, open the New dropdown and choose **Design / Web project**. Give the new page the client's project name. Set Client Link, Service Package, Target Outcome, Success Measure and Approval Contact.
3. Open the project page and complete Discovery brief and Proposal & scope builder. Agree the deliverables, exclusions, revision allowance, fee and dates with the client. Check Scope Agreed only after confirmation. Record the fee and dates in the project properties; use View details to open the property panel.
4. In **Focus desk → All tasks**, create the actual deliveries. Link each task to its project, choose Work Type = Delivery and set its deadline. Done closes a delivery task. The gallery shows progress based on delivery items, excluding change requests.
5. Record an invoice under Money desk. Select Client Link and Project Link. Enter its actual amount and due date. Update Amount Received when money arrives; Paid means fully paid. Amounts use the currency you agree with the client; do not mix currencies in a total.

## A change without a scope argument

Use **Scope Guard → Capture request**. Each request is one row in the existing Tasks source, with Work Type = Change request. Inside an existing project's Change desk, the Project Link filter scopes requests to that project.

Capture request keeps the entry screen short. Switch to **Estimate & approval** to record Extra Fee, Extra Hours, Deadline Impact Days and approval details. Check Estimate Reviewed when the estimate is complete, including an estimate of zero. Send the editable change-summary draft to the client yourself.

Only **Decision = Approved**, **Estimate Reviewed**, **Approval Evidence** and **Decision On** together make Change Gate = Approved. Approved Extra Fee/Hours then contribute to the project's Approved Extras/Approved Extra Hours. Declined and deferred requests remain in the history but do not contribute. A URL is a reference you record, not an electronic signature or verified authorization.

Approval does not alter the invoice, fee or deadline automatically. Create a separate Delivery task for the approved work, adjust the agreed dates and record the additional invoice as appropriate. This keeps the approval history separate from the work being delivered.

## Worked example — illustrative, not entered into your records

A client asks for an additional landing page after scope approval. You estimate 200 in the agreed currency, three hours and two extra days. Before approval the totals stay zero. After written confirmation is recorded with a decision date and the estimate is marked reviewed, Approved Extra Fee becomes 200 and Approved Extra Hours becomes 3. You then create the delivery task and manually update the invoice and deadline. Declining the request leaves those approved totals at zero.

## Handoff

The project kit includes a design/launch checklist, handoff asset register, client handoff email and debrief. Review the final work against the acceptance checks. Record client acceptance before checking Handoff Accepted. Reconcile outstanding invoices separately; handoff does not mean payment has arrived.

## Start from the example

Website Refresh, Alex Morgan and the original amounts/dates are fictional starter records. Inspect them to understand links; replace them with your information when you are ready. Blank new properties are intentional and do not claim an agreement has already happened.

## Keep client access deliberate

The master workspace is internal. Filtered views are not access control. Prepare a reviewed handoff document and choose its permissions before sharing anything with a client. Store credentials in a password manager, not in project notes.

## Client Experience Studio

Open **Client Experience Studio** from the home page. It includes a blank portal master, a fictional Northstar portal, a worked project and **Your first client portal**, an independent blank copy ready to configure.

Each portal has its own Content inbox, Review room and Decision log sources. Their working views appear inside the corresponding sections; source databases are under **Portal data / advanced**. Duplicate the whole portal page, keeping its sources inside the copy. A native copy within this workspace was checked: its six views reference three new independent sources, and its records start empty. Copying into a buyer's different workspace still needs a separate check.

For each required content item, record an asset URL and received date. **Checked** means the asset was reviewed for suitability. Received files remain **Needs review** until checked; a checkbox without a file/date cannot mark them ready.

For reviews, use a new row for each version and round. Record the preview URL, consolidated feedback, decision, reviewer and date. Approved rows missing the version, preview, reviewer or date remain **Complete approval record**. The gate records what was entered; it does not verify identity or collect an electronic signature.

Set **Scope check = Estimate needed** for work outside the baseline. Follow the studio guide to create a Change request in the internal project. Portal review decisions do not automatically authorize fees, create delivery tasks, change dates or issue invoices. Keep internal evidence and cost estimates out of the client portal.

The Northstar portal demonstrates missing content, an asset needing review, a superseded version, an approved version and a request outside scope. Its links use example.com placeholders, and all example people, dates and amounts are fictional. Its separate records do not contribute to internal business totals.

## What is manual

Client contact, approval evidence, invoice sending, payment reconciliation, deadline changes and publishing. There are no external payment, email or hosting integrations configured.

## Before using a duplicate

Check that Client Link, Project Link, Task Entries and the project-scoped views refer to the duplicated sources. Verify the New menu contains the two project/task templates. Test the workflow on your own example before using it for a real engagement. Mobile-app rendering and buyer duplication are release checks; a narrow desktop window is not a mobile-app test.
