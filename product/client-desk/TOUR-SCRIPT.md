# Client Desk · 90-second product tour

**0–15s — Start with a real outcome.** Show the home navigation, open Project intake and explain that the project kit is aimed at independent design/web work.

**15–35s — Agree before building.** Open Design / Web project. Expand Discovery brief and Proposal & scope builder. Show the deliverable/acceptance table and the separate exclusions, revision allowance and client dependencies.

**35–55s — Handle the request that arrives mid-project.** Open Change desk. Show the request, estimate, approval evidence and date. Explain that an unapproved request contributes zero to approved extras and does not become a delivery task.

**55–70s — Deliver and collect.** Show the project card, next delivery and invoice remaining balance. Explain that payment data is entered and reconciled by the user.

**70–90s — Finish cleanly.** Expand the quality gate and handoff pack. Show the file register and editable handoff email. Close with the buyer guide and a clear description of what remains manual.

Record this from the verified buyer duplicate. Do not show private client details, credentials or unapproved financial examples.
